What AI Can and Cannot Do (Demo)

You are an AI assistant supporting university staff.

List 5 tasks you are well-suited to help with in a university office setting, 
and 5 tasks you should NOT be used for.

Present the result as a two-column table.

Single-Step Prompt Example (Email)

You are a student services coordinator.

Write a clear, friendly email reminding undergraduate students 
about an upcoming registration deadline.

Constraints:
- 120 words or less
- Plain language
- Supportive but professional tone
- Include the deadline date and where to get help

Rewrite a Vague Prompt (Practice)

Rewrite the following vague prompt using Role, Task, Output, and Constraints:

"Summarize this policy."

Include:
- A specific audience
- A word limit
- A tone appropriate for a university setting

Multi-Step Prompt Template

You are an AI assistant supporting university staff.

Step 1: Analyze the goal of the task and identify key information needed.
Step 2: Generate a first draft of the content.
Step 3: Review the draft and improve clarity, tone, and usability 
for the intended audience.

Pause after each step and wait for confirmation before continuing.

Instructor Demo: Student FAQ

You are a communications specialist at a university.

Step 1: Identify the top 6 questions students may have about a new 
campus policy requiring ID cards for building access.

Step 2: Write short, clear answers using student-friendly language.

Step 3: Revise the answers to reduce jargon and student anxiety.

Guided Practice: Build a 3-Step Prompt

Choose a common task from your role (email, document, checklist, report).

Write a 3-step prompt where:
- Step 1 analyzes or gathers information
- Step 2 creates a draft
- Step 3 revises or adapts it for a specific audience

Draft → Review → Revise Pattern

You are an administrative coordinator.

Step 1: Draft an internal announcement about a change in office hours.
Step 2: Review the draft for clarity and conciseness.
Step 3: Revise it for posting on the staff intranet.

Policy Summary for Staff

You are a policy analyst supporting university staff.

Step 1: Summarize the key points of the policy in bullet form.
Step 2: Translate the summary into plain language.
Step 3: Rewrite the information as a short FAQ.

Meeting Notes to Follow-Up Email

You are an executive assistant.

Step 1: Identify action items, decisions, and open questions 
from these meeting notes.
Step 2: Organize them by owner and deadline.
Step 3: Draft a clear follow-up email summarizing next steps.

Capstone Lab: Role-Based Multi-Step Prompt

You are an AI assistant supporting my role at the university.

Step 1: Ask me clarifying questions about the task I want to complete.
Step 2: Create a first draft based on my responses.
Step 3: Improve the draft for clarity, accuracy, and audience fit.
Step 4: Suggest one way I could reuse this prompt in the future.
