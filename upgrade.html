Prelude — Copilot Memory Prompt

Copilot Working Preferences Memory

Remember the following information about how I work and the kinds of help I want from Copilot:
• I work in a university administrative/professional environment.
• Much of my work involves creating executive-ready documents, emails, analysis, and presentations for leadership.
• I prefer outputs that are structured, concise, and ready to share with stakeholders.
• When possible, tailor responses for different audiences such as students, faculty, administrators, or executives.
• I value clear next steps, decision points, and action items.
• Assume I am comfortable with Microsoft Word, Outlook, Excel, and PowerPoint and want advanced, time-saving help.
Use this to improve your future responses for me across Microsoft 365 apps.

Module 1 — Advanced Word

Scenario 1: Policy Draft from Messy Notes

Act as a university policy analyst with expertise in writing formal institutional policies. Transform this document into a professional Student Travel Fund Policy using this structure:
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
2. ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
3. AWARD LIMITS (table format)
4. APPLICATION PROCESS
5. ADVANCE PAYMENT PROVISIONS
6. POST-TRAVEL REQUIREMENTS
7. REVIEW AND APPEALS
For unresolved items, insert [COMMITTEE DECISION NEEDED]. Use formal policy language. The audience is the Provost’s Office.

Scenario 2A: Student Announcement

Based on this document, write a student-friendly announcement (max 250 words) highlighting what changes benefit them directly. Use an enthusiastic but professional tone. Include an FAQ with the 3 questions students are most likely to ask. Format for email with a subject line.

Scenario 2B: Faculty Communication

Write a communication for faculty (max 300 words) focusing on syllabus planning, exam scheduling, and grade submission changes. Include a timeline of key dates and address concerns about the compressed exam period.

Scenario 2C: Administrative Operations Memo

Create an operational memo for department administrators (max 400 words). Focus on systems updates, coordination needs, and ownership. Include a responsibility matrix. Use formal memo formatting.

Module 2 — Advanced Outlook

Scenario 3: Complex Planning Update Email

Draft a planning update email that:
1. Opens positively
2. Categorizes issues as RESOLVED, NEEDS INPUT, RISKS
3. Presents exactly two options with pros/cons for each decision
4. Solves poster overflow
5. Includes a budget decision matrix
6. Ends with clear asks and a deadline
Maintain a calm, solutions-oriented tone.

Scenario 4: Email Thread Synthesis

Analyze this email thread and produce:
DECISIONS MADE
OPEN QUESTIONS
CONFLICTING POSITIONS
RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS
ACTION ITEMS ASSIGNED
ITEMS THAT FELL THROUGH THE CRACKS
Use bullet points. Flag urgency.
