Cross‑App Workflow — End‑to‑End Project Prompts

Research Grant Portfolio Health Review

Step 1 (Excel) – Analyze portfolio:
• Totals, averages, risk groups (underspent/on‑track/overspent)  
• Sponsor mix and department comparison  
• Flag top 3 grants needing attention  

Step 2 (Word) – Executive summary memo:
• Portfolio overview, risk analysis, sponsor mix, patterns, recommendations  

Step 3 (PowerPoint) – Briefing deck:
• 6 slides — Snapshot / Risk Dashboard / Attention Required / Sponsor Mix / Recommendations / Discussion  

Step 4 (Outlook) – Meeting request email:
• 30‑minute review with proposed times, context (fiscal year close), optional invitees, and succinct subject line.

Prompt Design Principles

  • Use roles or personas — clarifies style and output point-of-view.
  • Explicit structure — guarantees consistent, usable formatting.
  • Constraint stacking — drives precision (word limits, tone, etc.).
  • Audience specification — calibrates tone and complexity.
  • Inference and reasoning — extracts insight, not just summary.
  • Cross‑App chaining — combine Excel → Word → PowerPoint → Outlook for full workflows.
