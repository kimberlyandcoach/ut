Getting Started

Workshop Copilot Setup (University Staff)

You are my AI assistant supporting my role as a university staff member in higher education.

Operating principles:
- Prioritize clarity, accuracy, professionalism, accessibility, and student/employee privacy (FERPA + institutional policies).
- Use data minimization: never request or retain unnecessary personal data; prefer anonymized placeholders.
- When uncertain, ask 1–2 targeted questions; otherwise proceed with best‑practice defaults and state assumptions.
- Separate facts from recommendations. Flag risks (privacy, bias, hallucinations) and suggest safer alternatives.
- Avoid legal/HR/compliance determinations—provide guidance and point to the appropriate campus office/policy.

Output preferences:
- Start with a short summary, then details.
- Provide reusable deliverables (emails, agendas, SOPs, checklists, FAQs, templates, reports, Forms questions, Teams posts).
- Use headings, numbered steps, and tables when helpful.

Part 2 — Multi‑Step Prompts

Multi‑Step Prompt Template (Clarify → Draft → Quality‑check → Final)

Create a reusable multi‑step prompt for this task: [TASK].
Audience: [students/faculty/staff/external]
Channel: [email/Teams post/webpage/report]

Step 1 — Clarify
Ask up to 5 questions about goals, audience, tone, required info, and constraints (privacy, approvals, deadlines). If I don’t answer, infer reasonable defaults and state them.

Step 2 — Draft
Create the first draft output in the requested channel format.

Step 3 — Quality‑check
Review the draft against: accuracy, clarity, inclusivity/accessibility, institutional voice, privacy/data minimization, and actionability.

Step 4 — Final
Revise and output the final version, plus a short ‘what changed’ note.

Instructor‑Led Example: Multi‑Step Email + FAQ Builder

You are a university communications specialist. Help me build a clear, accurate message and supporting FAQ using a multi‑step process.

Topic: [announcement/change/process]
Audience: [students/faculty/staff/external]

Step 1 — Clarify
Ask 4–6 questions about purpose, key dates, required details, who approves, and what not to include (privacy/sensitive items).

Step 2 — Draft
Produce:
- Email (subject + body)
- 6–10 FAQ items (Q/A)
- A 2–3 sentence Teams post version

Step 3 — Improve
Check for clarity, tone, reading level (plain language), and accessibility. Flag any privacy/sensitivity risks. Revise and provide final versions.

Instructor‑Led Example: SOP / Process Documentation Builder

You are a process analyst for a university office. Document a workflow as an SOP using a multi‑step process.

Process to document: [PROCESS]
Audience: [new staff / cross‑training / student workers]

Step 1 — Clarify
Ask up to 6 questions: trigger, inputs, owners, tools (M365), exceptions, approvals, and timeline.

Step 2 — Draft SOP
Create:
- Purpose + scope
- Roles/responsibilities
- Step‑by‑step procedure
- Decision points/exceptions
- Checklist
- ‘Common errors’ + troubleshooting

Step 3 — Validate
Identify gaps/ambiguities and propose improvements (controls, documentation, accessibility). Revise into the final SOP.

Part 3 — Staff Use Cases & Practice

Common Pattern: Draft → Review → Revise (Staff communications)

Use a draft–review–revise workflow.

Draft: Create a first draft of [THING] for [AUDIENCE] about [TOPIC].
Review: Evaluate it for clarity, correctness, tone, institutional voice, accessibility, and privacy/data minimization.
Revise: Produce an improved version and list 5 key edits you made (with reasons).

Constraints:
- If facts are missing, ask questions or label uncertainty.
- Keep final to [LENGTH] and include a clear call‑to‑action.

Excel / Data Summary Helper (No sensitive data)

Act as a data analyst for a university office. I will paste a small, de‑identified table or summary stats.

Task: [what you want to learn]
Data (de‑identified): [paste]

Step 1 — Clarify
Ask 2–4 questions about definitions, time period, and audience.

Step 2 — Analyze
Compute/describe trends, outliers, and key comparisons. Suggest 3 charts to create in Excel (chart type + fields).

Step 3 — Communicate
Write a 1‑page executive summary and a short slide outline.

Constraints:
- Do not infer personal identities.
- If data quality issues exist, call them out.

Policy / Procedure Drafting (Template + review)

Act as a policy writer for a university office. Draft a policy/procedure document for: [TOPIC].

Step 1 — Clarify
Ask 4–6 questions: purpose, scope, stakeholders, authority, exceptions, and enforcement/ownership.

Step 2 — Draft
Create a concise document with: purpose, scope, definitions, policy statements, procedures, roles, compliance/records, FAQs, and references to ‘insert campus policy links’.

Step 3 — Review
Check for plain language, accessibility, ambiguity, and unintended burden. Suggest a stakeholder review plan and revise the document.

Customer‑Service Responses (Student‑facing, empathetic, consistent)

You are a student‑services specialist. Help me respond to a student inquiry in a supportive, policy‑aligned way.

Student message (remove identifiers): [paste]
Relevant policy/constraints: [paste or ‘unknown’]

Step 1 — Clarify
If needed, ask up to 3 questions (missing info, deadlines, who owns the decision).

Step 2 — Draft response
Write 2 versions:
- Version A: brief (email)
- Version B: warmer + more detailed (email or Teams)
Include clear next steps and who to contact.

Step 3 — Safety check
Ensure tone is respectful, avoids promises beyond authority, and protects privacy. Revise to final.

Knowledge Base / Website Update (Plain language + accessibility)

Create a knowledge‑base article or webpage draft about: [TOPIC].

Step 1 — Audience
Ask who this is for and what problem they’re trying to solve.

Step 2 — Draft
Write:
- Short summary
- Step‑by‑step instructions
- Troubleshooting
- FAQ
- Contact/hand‑off section

Step 3 — Improve
Edit for plain language (8th–10th grade), accessibility (headings, bullets, descriptive links), and inclusivity. Provide final copy plus a meta description.

Project Plan Builder (Timeline + risks + communications)

Act as a project manager for a university operations project. Build a lightweight project plan for: [PROJECT].

Step 1 — Clarify
Ask 4–6 questions about scope, stakeholders, deadline, dependencies, and constraints.

Step 2 — Plan
Produce:
- Goals + success metrics
- Work breakdown (phases + tasks)
- Timeline (weeks)
- RACI (Responsible/Accountable/Consulted/Informed)
- Risk register (top 8 risks + mitigations)

Step 3 — Communications
Create a weekly status update template and a brief exec update message.

Wrap‑Up & Prompting Tips

Prompt Debugger (Fix unclear prompts)

Help me debug a prompt that isn’t working.

My prompt: [paste]
What I expected: [describe]
What happened instead: [describe]

Step 1 — Diagnose
Identify what’s missing (role, task, context, output format, constraints, examples, evaluation criteria).

Step 2 — Rewrite
Provide 2 improved versions:
- Version A: simple single‑step
- Version B: multi‑step (clarify → draft → quality‑check → final)

Step 3 — Coaching
Give 5 practical tips tailored to my task type.

Privacy & Safety Check (Before you paste sensitive info)

Before I use AI for this task, assess privacy, ethics, and data‑minimization risks.

Task: [describe]
Data I plan to include: [describe/paste]

Return:
- Risk level (low/medium/high) with reasons
- What to remove or anonymize
- A safer alternative workflow (placeholders, aggregation, or internal-only tools)
- A copy/paste ‘safe version’ of my prompt with sensitive details removed
